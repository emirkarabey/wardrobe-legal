MyWardrobe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

MyWardrobe is a mobile app that digitises your wardrobe and suggests outfits using AI. This policy explains what data is processed when you use it, why, who it is shared with, and how you delete it.

1. Who is responsible, and how to reach us

The app is developed and operated by Emir Karabey. For any question, request or complaint about privacy: emir.karabey1@hotmail.com

3. About your photos and the body photo

The garment and body photos you upload, and the try-on images made from them, are processed only to provide the service to you. They are never used to train any AI model, never used for testing or research, and never shown to other users. A try-on image leaves the app only when you tap “Share”.

Photos are sent to a single AI provider, Google Gemini: garment photos for tagging, your body photo and garments for generating try-on images. Google does not use content sent to its paid API service to improve its products or train models; it may keep a request for a limited time to detect abuse.

  • Your body photo is used only to show your own outfits on you.
  • It is sent to the AI provider solely to generate that image; it is not used to train any model.
  • It is held in private storage that only your account can reach. Access is granted through short-lived signed links.
  • You can delete it from inside the app at any time; the file itself is removed with it.
  • You may not upload a photo of another person without their explicit consent, and photos of anyone under 16 may not be uploaded at all.

Email addresses and photo links are stripped from crash reports, and no screenshots or view hierarchies are sent.

5. How long it is kept

Data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your wardrobe, outfits, try-on images and body photo are deleted permanently. Purchase records may be retained in anonymised form for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

6. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and GDPR arts. 15–22 you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection.

  • Erasure: in the app, Profile → “Delete my account”. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Deleting only the body photo: Profile → Body photo → Delete.
  • Deleting try-on images: Today → My try-ons → Select. Delete one, several or all; the image itself is removed.
  • Anything else: emir.karabey1@hotmail.com — answered within 30 days at the latest.

7. Children

The app is not designed for, and is not marketed to, anyone under 16. If we learn that we hold data belonging to someone under 16, we delete it without delay.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted with TLS. Every row in the database is bound to your account by row-level security, so no other user can reach your data. Session keys are held in the device's secure store (the iOS Keychain).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. You will be told inside the app about any material change.
